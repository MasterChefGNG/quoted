quoted for Android

Hamlet quote tester for Leaving Cert English

Download
  1. Tap Download. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Tap Open when it finishes (or find quoted.apk in your Downloads).
  3. If your phone says it can't install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway. It warns about any app that isn't from the Play Store.

After that, it updates itself. When there's a new version, quoted shows it when you open the app, and one tap gets it. Your progress is kept.
